### Core Research and Program Activities

Glenn's core technical research and service focuses on applied flood risk science and regulatory flood mapping for the state of Illinois. His work directly supports FEMA's National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) by:

- Directing the production of updated Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRMs) for Illinois communities, incorporating updated topographic data (LiDAR), updated hydrologic and hydraulic analyses, and modern risk communication products
- Advancing the integration of "Future Risk" data alongside regulatory flood maps, allowing communities to see not just current FEMA-designated flood zones but modeled future-condition flood risk under climate change and development scenarios
- Developing and implementing AI-assisted workflows for H&H analysis review, program tracking, and regulatory compliance documentation within CHAMP
- Leading state-level coordination with IDNR on flood risk data management and the IIWIC statewide data infrastructure initiative
- Serving as Illinois' liaison to FEMA Region 5 and contributing to national CTP program coordination through ASFPM and FEMA TMAC
