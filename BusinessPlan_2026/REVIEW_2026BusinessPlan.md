# Editorial & Content Review — 2026 Illinois CTP Business Plan
**Reviewed file:** `2026StateILJointBusinessPlan_June2026-May2027.docx`  
**Comparison file:** `2026StateILJointBusinessPlan_OWR Updates.docx`  
**Reviewed by:** CHAMP-PM Agent  
**Date:** 2026-06-15  

---

## Summary

The main draft is substantially more complete than the OWR Updates version (545 lines added vs. 56 removed). The document is largely sound, but has **two high-priority errors**, **several stale-date issues**, and **one significant structural problem (duplicate content block)**. Detailed findings below, organized by severity.

---

## 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY — Must Fix Before Submission

### 1. Table 10 Year Mismatch (Section 2 vs. Section 3)

**Location:** Capital Appropriations body text (appears in *both* the IDNR/OWR section overview and the Staffing section)

**Problem:** The body text says:
> *"IDNR/OWR requested Re-Appropriations for the **fiscal year 2022** shown in Table 10..."*

But the table caption in the ToC now reads:
> *"Table 10. FY26 OWR Requested Re-Appropriations"*

The table caption was updated from FY22 to FY26 (visible in the diff vs. OWR Updates), but the **prose was never updated**. This is a direct year contradiction.

**Fix:** Change "fiscal year 2022" → "fiscal year 2026" in both instances of the Capital Appropriations paragraph.

---

### 2. Duplicate Content Block — Engineering Studies / Technical Services / Capital Appropriations

**Location:** Appears first in the IDNR/OWR overview paragraphs (before the org chart), then again verbatim in the Division of Capital Programs section.

The following three subsections appear **word-for-word twice**:
- Engineering Studies Section (bullet list of activities)
- Technical Services Section (activities related to flooding)
- Capital Appropriations (Table 10 reference paragraph)

One copy is slightly different — the second (Staffing section) copy adds:
> *"Bathymetric surveys of water supply lakes and State-managed recreational impoundments"*

and also has the misspelling `survesy` (see below). The first copy omits this line.

**Fix:** Decide which version is authoritative and remove the duplicate. The Staffing section copy should be the keeper (it has the additional line); remove the earlier duplicate block. Also fix the misspelling.

- *"AECOM (Construction Inspection Consultants – contract thru **2-28-2026**)"*

### 8. Stale Staffing Retirement/Hire Dates

**Location:** Statewide Programs Section staffing
> *"2 Floodplain Managers (2 CFM (1 starting **10/1/2025**), 1 CFM (retiring **12/31/25** (to be backfilled))"*

These past-tense events are presented as future. The new hire started 10/1/2025 (8+ months ago); the retiring CFM left 12/31/2025. Update to reflect current actual staffing.

---

### 9. Statewide 2D BLE — Only a Footnote, No Narrative Section

**Location:** Table 7 footnote 1
> *"Starting in FY26, ISWS intends to initiate a multi-year statewide 2D BLE project..."*

This is a **major new program initiative** but it only appears as a table footnote. There is no narrative description of the 2D BLE program in Section 2 (Project Planning). Given its significance and multi-year scope, it should have at least a paragraph in the Section 2 narrative — similar to how the Kishwaukee phasing plan is described.

---

### 10. Table of Contents Page Numbers Appear Stale (vs. OWR Updates)

**Comparison finding:** The OWR Updates version had different page numbers throughout:
- OWR: Section 2 at page 17, Section 3 at page 37, Section 4 at page 58
- Main: Section 2 at page 16, Section 3 at page 35, Section 4 at page 56

Page numbers shifted by ~2 pages. This suggests content was added/removed between versions. **Verify the ToC page numbers are regenerated/updated** before submission.

## 🟡 LOW PRIORITY — Consider Updating

### 11. Appendix LiDAR Acquisition Text Is 6 Years Stale

**Location:** Appendix section

Multiple paragraphs describe LiDAR acquisition as ongoing "in the process of being acquired in Winter 2019/2020" and "if flooding conditions subside before leaf-out." These events are ~6 years past. The Appendix should be updated to reflect the current status (LiDAR is now available statewide per Figure 7/8).

---

### 12. Most Recent Publication Cited Is from 2020

**Location:** Showcase of Recent Accomplishments → Recent ISWS Publications
> *"Precipitation Frequency Study for Illinois. **2020**..."*
> *"Structure-Specific Flood Risk Assessment Studies, **2019**..."*

In a 2026 business plan, these are 6–7 years old. If ISWS has produced any relevant publications since 2020, they should be added. At minimum, consider noting whether Bulletin 75 has been updated.

---

### 13. CTP Recognition Award Reference Is Now 5 Years Old

**Location:** Showcase of Recent Accomplishments
> *"In **2021**, FEMA recognized the Illinois Department of Natural Resources and Illinois State Water Survey for the fifth annual CTP Recognition Award..."*

This is fine as historical context, but the section is titled "Showcase of **Recent** Accomplishments." If there are more recent awards or recognition events (2022–2025), they should be added. Otherwise, consider renaming this section or framing the 2021 award as a lasting achievement.

---

### 14. Community Outreach Section Uses Hedging Language ("Potential Actions Identified")

**Location:** Section 4 — Community Outreach and Mitigation Strategies

Several subsections list actions under "Potential Actions Identified:" without indicating which have been implemented. For a business plan submitted to FEMA, it would be stronger to frame completed actions as achievements and framed planned actions as commitments, rather than everything as "potential."

---

### 15. Floodplain Mapping Study Reviews — Activities Period May Need Update

**Location:** Floodplain Mapping Study Reviews → "In summary, this section's **June 2023 - May 2024** activities included:"

This is a one-year-old reporting period. Should be updated to June 2024 - May 2025 for the current plan cycle, with updated statistics.

---

### 16. "Recommendations to FEMA" — Listed in ToC But Has No Body Content

**Location:** Section 4 ToC entry, Section 4 body

The ToC lists "Recommendations to FEMA" as a section heading, but no substantive recommendations appear under that heading in the body text. Either add content or remove the heading from the ToC.
