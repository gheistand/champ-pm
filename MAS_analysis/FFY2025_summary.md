# FFY2025 MAS Portfolio Summary (DRAFT — UNFUNDED)

## Overview

The ISWS CHAMP program proposed **14 draft MAS/SOW documents** for FFY2025, covering a total proposed FEMA contribution of approximately **$3,586,000**. These documents were prepared for submission under an anticipated FFY2025 NOFO that was never issued due to federal budget/political constraints. No work was awarded under these MASs. Because they represent fully-drafted, staffed proposals with defined scopes and budgets, they are the most likely baseline for FFY2026 application development.

The portfolio spans five types of work:
- **Program administration** (PM SOW, COMS SOW, LOMR) — 3 documents, ~$645,000
- **Flood Risk Products** supplements to projects nearing effectiveness (Peoria, McHenry, McDonough) — 3 documents, ~$110,470
- **Post-Preliminary / Final processing** toward effective FIRMs (Edwards, Wabash, Macoupin) — 3 documents, ~$510,170
- **Preliminary Map Products** production (Montgomery, Wayne) — 2 documents, ~$409,360
- **Data Development** for new/updated flood studies (Kishwaukee Phase 6, Boone County, Saline HUC8) — 3 documents, ~$1,911,000

All projects have FEMA RPO John Wethington (Region 5). Glenn Heistand is PM for all technical mapping MASs; Brian Chaille is PM for the LOMR MAS.

### 3. Budget Scale and NOFO Uncertainty
The full portfolio totals ~$3,586,000 in FEMA funding requests. FEMA Region 5 CTP awards historically vary by year and NOFO priorities. If FFY2026 funding is constrained, the Tier 1 and Tier 2 projects represent the most "sunk cost recovery" per dollar (projects far along the pipeline that stall without this funding). Kishwaukee Phase 6 is the biggest single risk if deprioritized — a $1.4M project that is the keystone for Preliminary mapping for multiple Northern Illinois counties.

### 4. Contractor Needs
MASs 25-11 (Kishwaukee) and 25-12 (Boone County) both list contractor as **TBD**. Procurement planning should begin immediately once FFY2026 NOFO is confirmed, as competitive procurement can take 3–6 months and these are technically complex, multi-year contracts.

### 5. Levee Flags
Two projects note National Levee Database (NLD) levees in their study areas:
- **ISWS-25-07 Wabash County** — multiple NLD-listed levees; accreditation status must be tracked
- **ISWS-25-10 Wayne County** — multiple NLD-listed levees; accreditation status must be tracked
Levee accreditation issues can add time and cost to due process phases. These should be verified against current NLD status before FFY2026 submission.

### 6. FRP Supplement Dependencies
Projects 25-03, 25-04, and 25-05 are only executable if their predecessor FFY2024 mapping MASs (ISWS 24-03, 24-04, 24-05) successfully complete the regulatory FIRM to effective. If any of those predecessors are delayed, the FRP supplement timeline shifts accordingly. These supplements are relatively inexpensive ($35–40K each) and can be deferred without major program impact if the predecessor is behind schedule.

### 7. Period of Performance Notes
- All FRP/MAS documents: 3-year PoP (through 09/30/2028 if starting 10/01/2025)
- PM SOW, COMS SOW, LOMR: 2-year PoP
- For FFY2026 re-submission: shift PoP start to 10/01/2026; end dates adjust to 09/30/2029 (3-yr) and 09/30/2028 (2-yr)
