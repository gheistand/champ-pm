# FFY2026 Recommended Project Portfolio
## Illinois State Water Survey — CHAMP Program

*Prepared from analysis of FFY2020–FFY2024 funded portfolios and the draft (unfunded) FFY2025 portfolio.*
*Context: FFY2025 NOFO was never issued due to federal budget/political constraints. This document recommends FFY2026 projects with the FFY2025 draft MASs as the primary baseline, updated for a 12-month period-of-performance shift and current project status.*

---

## Executive Summary

The ISWS CHAMP program enters FFY2026 with a one-year funding gap that has created five stalled near-final projects and a growing backlog of data development work awaiting production funding. The program has six effective FIRMs delivered across the FFY2020–FFY2022 cycle (Clay, Effingham, Stark, Bond, Richland, Cook/Kane PMR). Three more (Peoria, McHenry Nippersink, McDonough) are completing Post-Preliminary Processing under active FFY2024 awards and will issue effective FIRMs in 2025–2026 without additional production funding.

The FFY2026 portfolio must accomplish three things simultaneously:
1. **Unstall five near-complete projects** (Edwards, Wabash, Macoupin, and the upcoming Post-Preliminary MAS needs for Johnson and Bureau Counties).
2. **Advance the mid-pipeline investments** (Montgomery, Wayne, Kishwaukee Phase 6, Boone County) that are data-ready but have not yet entered the regulatory mapping process.
3. **Address the southern Illinois data development backlog** (Massac/Pope, Hamilton, White County, Rock Island) where KDP-2 data is aging without a production MAS.

**Recommended FFY2026 portfolio total: approximately $4,700,000–$5,200,000** (core program + recommended Tier 3 items). If NOFO funding is constrained below $4.5M, Tier 3 projects should be reduced in scope before cutting Tier 1 or Tier 2 items.

**Period of Performance:** All FRP MASs: 10/01/2026 – 09/30/2029 (3-year). PM, COMS, and LOMR SOWs: 10/01/2026 – 09/30/2028 (2-year).

### Proposed ISWS_26-11: Kishwaukee HUC8 Watershed — Data Development Phase 6 (Final DD Phase)

- **Prior Funded Work:** Discovery (ISWS_17-02) → Phase 1 survey (ISWS_20-12) → Phase 2 mainstem H&H (ISWS_21-12) → Phase 3 survey DeKalb/McHenry (ISWS_22-09) → Phase 4 H&H Winnebago/Boone/Ogle (ISWS_23-08, active) → Phase 5 tributaries DeKalb/Kane/McHenry (ISWS_24-08, active)
- **Current Phase:** Phases 4 and 5 actively executing under FFY2023 and FFY2024 awards. Phase 6 is the **explicitly described "final data development phase"** before Preliminary mapping begins.
- **Proposed FFY2026 Scope:**
  - New Zone A and Zone AE studies for ~195 miles of streams in Ogle, DeKalb, Kane, and McHenry Counties
  - 99 mi updated effective Zone A / 2D modeling; 67 mi updated Zone AE; 19 mi new unmapped Zone A; 10 mi new detailed Zone AE
  - Hydrology: HEC-HMS, USGS Regression, Bulletin 17C, and/or 2D Rain on Mesh
  - Hydraulics: 1D and 2D HEC-RAS
  - Draft FIRM Database production (outputs to QR1-ready database)
  - Community engagement: PICC Meeting, Flood Risk Review Meeting, Resilience Meeting
  - SID 620/621 regulatory notifications
  - Deliverables to MIP
- **Estimated Budget:** $1,414,000 (directly from FFY2025 draft ISWS_25-11)
- **Study Miles:** 195 total
- **Deliverable:** Completed data development + Draft FIRM Database for Ogle, DeKalb, Kane, McHenry County portions of Kishwaukee HUC8
- **Contractor Note:** TBD — procurement must begin immediately upon NOFO award. Competitive procurement typically takes 3–6 months.
- **FFY2027 Link:** Phase 6 completion unlocks Preliminary DFIRM MASs for DeKalb County, Kane County, McHenry County, and/or Ogle County (likely as two or three separate county-level PMR production MASs).
- **Rationale:** Eight grant years of watershed investment will be stranded without Phase 6. Phase 6 is the keystone that converts data into regulatory products. Serves major Northern Illinois suburban communities (DeKalb, Sycamore, Woodstock, Crystal Lake, Elgin, Marengo, Huntley, Algonquin, and more).
