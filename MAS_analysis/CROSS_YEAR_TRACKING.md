## Key Cross-Year Observations

### 1. Five Projects Fully Complete (Effective FIRM Issued)
Clay County, Effingham County, Stark County, Bond County, Richland County, and Cook/Kane PMR have all reached effective FIRM. These represent successful completion of the full lifecycle investment.

### 2. Three Projects Approaching Effective FIRM (FFY2024 Terminal MAS Active)
Peoria, McHenry (Nippersink), and McDonough County are all in Post-Preliminary Processing under FFY2024 awards. They should issue effective FIRMs in 2025–2026. The only FFY2026 action needed is funding the small FRP supplements ($35–40K each).

### 3. Five Projects Stalled Without FFY2025 Funding
Edwards County and Wabash County are frozen at post-Open House awaiting Due Process funding. Macoupin County has a completed, reviewed Preliminary FIRM sitting undistributed. Johnson County and Bureau County are completing Open House under FFY2024 awards and will need Post-Preliminary MASs immediately after. All five are ready to proceed and represent the highest-priority FFY2026 investments.

### 4. Kishwaukee Watershed Is the Defining Long-Term Commitment
Eight MASs across seven grant years (FFY2017–FFY2024, plus FFY2025 draft) have been invested in the Kishwaukee HUC8 watershed. Phase 6 is the final data development phase and the gateway to Preliminary DFIRM for 4–6 Northern Illinois counties. The $1.4M Phase 6 MAS is non-negotiable for the FFY2026 portfolio.

### 5. Data Development Backlog in Southern Illinois
Massac, Pope, Hamilton, and White Counties all completed data development between FFY2020 and FFY2022 — 4–6 years ago — with no production MAS funded since. This data is aging and DFIRM production MASs are overdue.

### 6. Green River / Bureau-Henry-Whiteside Corridor Nearing Production
Bureau County is in Preliminary production (FFY2024). Henry County and Whiteside County have QR1 databases plus new supplemental data development (FFY2023). All three share the Green River watershed. After the FFY2023 supplemental data development completes, a coordinated set of Preliminary production MASs for Henry and Whiteside Counties would be timely.
